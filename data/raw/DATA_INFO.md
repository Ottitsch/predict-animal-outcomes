Both files are the frozen City of Austin snapshots covering Oct 1st 2013 to May 5th 2025.
Their SHA256 digests are in `SHA256SUMS`; `data/prepare_data.py` refuses to run on a mismatch.

**Austin Animal Center Outcomes (10/01/2013 to 05/05/2025)**  
https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/austin-animal-center-outcomes  
https://data.austintexas.gov/d/9t4d-g238  
Metadata Updated: February 25, 2026  

Outcomes represent the status of animals as they leave the Animal Center. All animals receive a unique Animal ID during intake. Annually over 90% of animals entering the center, are adopted, transferred to rescue or returned to their owners. The Outcomes data set reflects that Austin, TX. is the largest "No Kill" city in the country.

**Austin Animal Center Intakes (10/01/2013 to 05/05/2025)**  
https://data.austintexas.gov/d/wter-evkm  
Downloaded November 18, 2026 as CSV.  

Intakes represent the status of animals as they arrive at the Animal Center. All animals receive a unique Animal ID during intake. An animal that comes back gets a new intake row under the same Animal ID.
